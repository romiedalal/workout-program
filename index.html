The Four Sessions

Lower A — Quad Emphasis
Sunday · Upstairs Gym
55–65 min Quads · Hams · Calves · Core
  1. Leg Press
    Primary quad driver. Add load when 10 reps clean across all sets.
    4 × 8–10 RIR 1–2
  2. Bulgarian Split Squat
    DBs 50–75 lb each. Long stance, knee tracks toes.
    3 × 8–10 / leg RIR 1
  3. Leg Extension
    Pause 1 sec at top. Stretched-position bias.
    4 × 12–15 RIR 0
  4. Lying / Seated Leg Curl
    Slow eccentric. Hams secondary today; main hinge work Thursday.
    4 × 10–12 RIR 1
  5. Standing Calf Raise
    Full ROM, pause stretch.
    4 × 12–15 RIR 0
  6. Hanging Leg Raise
    Strict, no swing.
    3 × AMRAP RIR 0
Weekly tally contribution → Quads 11 · Hams 4 · Calves 4 · Core 3
Upper A — BB Push + Ring Pull
Tuesday · Apartment
60–70 min Chest · Back · Biceps · Triceps
  1. Barbell Bench Press
    Start 135 lb. Add 1.25 lb plates when 8 × 4 sets clean. Pause reps when ≥ 180 lb.
    4 × 6–8 RIR 1–2
  2. Weighted Ring Pull-Up
    BW + 10 lb start. Full ROM, chest to rings.
    4 × 6–8 RIR 1
  3. Ring Dip
    Bodyweight. Add 18 lb KB in feet when easy.
    3 × 8–12 RIR 1
  4. Single-Arm KB Row
    72 lb KB. 3-sec eccentric. Alternating arms.
    3 × 10–12 / arm RIR 1
  5. Spider Curl on Rings
    Body angled, rings low. Squeeze hard at top.
    4 × 10–12 RIR 0
  6. Ring Skull-Crusher
    Body angled. Lower forehead behind head, drive up.
    3 × 12–15 RIR 0
Weekly tally contribution → Chest 7 · Back 7 · Biceps 4 · Triceps 6
Lower B — Hinge Emphasis
Thursday · Apartment
50–60 min Hams · Glutes · Calves · Core
  1. Romanian Deadlift
    3-sec eccentric from week 1. Bar caps at 222.5 lb — tempo carries the load.
    4 × 8–10 RIR 2
  2. Barbell Hip Thrust
    Couch + folded towel pad. Pause 1 sec at top.
    3 × 10–12 RIR 1
  3. Single-Leg KB RDL
    72 lb KB, opposite hand. Hips square.
    3 × 8–10 / leg RIR 1
  4. KB Swing
    72 lb hardstyle. Hip-hinge driven, not squat-driven. 2-min rest.
    4 × 10 RIR 2
  5. Seated Calf Raise
    KB on knees, toes off plate. Burns intentional.
    3 × 15–20 RIR 0
  6. Pallof Press
    Rings, anti-rotation. Hold 2 sec at full extension.
    3 × 10 / side RIR 1

Auto-Regulation — Run Every Morning

The 5-Question Check

Each YES = 1 point. Run before every lift session.

  1. Slept < 6 hrs last night?
  2. Resting HR > 7 bpm above baseline (Whoop)?
  3. Sparred or hard-contact boxing in last 24 hrs?
  4. Joint pain at end-range during warm-up (lead shoulder, hip, knee)?
  5. Subjective energy ≤ 4/10?
0–1
Train as planned. Standard session, push for progression.
2
Train with cap. Top sets at RIR 2 minimum. No PRs today.
3
Reduce. Cut top-set load by 30% OR working sets by 1. Keep movement.
4–5
Skip the lift. 20 min mobility + ring work. This rule protects your boxing.

Deload Weeks (6 · 12 · 18 · 24)

  • Sets: 50% of last week's volume.
  • Load: 70% of last week's working weight.
  • RIR: 4–5 across all sets. Should feel easy.
  • Boxing: Hold normal training. Skip sparring if optional.
  • Nutrition: Hold maintenance. Deload is not a cut.
