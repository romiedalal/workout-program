The Program.

Four lift sessions on rotation. Pick whichever days work — just leave 24 hrs between hitting the same muscles. Bag work fits anywhere, no special rules.

    Pause 1 sec at top.
    4 × 12–15 RIR 0

Mobility & Splits Program

24 weeks to splits. Daily warm-up + 5×/week dedicated stretching. Science-based, no fluff.

The science (skip pre-workout static stretching)
  • Static stretching pre-workout reduces strength for 60 min — use dynamic warm-up before lifting/running. Behm 2016, Sports Med.
  • Long holds 60–120 sec are 2× more effective than short ones for permanent ROM. Konrad 2024 meta.
  • PNF (contract 6s → relax + deepen 30s) = gold standard for fast flexibility. Sharman 2006.
  • Loaded stretching (Cossack squats, ATG split squats, weighted pancake) = strength + flexibility in one. Israetel / Nuckols / Knee-Ability ATG framework.
  • Daily > intensity for tissue remodeling. Kit Laughlin / Tsatsouline.
  • Heat first — warm shower or 5 min dynamic warm-up makes stretches 2–3× more effective. Behm & Chaouachi 2011.
  • Permanent ROM = 3–6 months of consistent work. Magnusson 1998+.

Daily warm-up (5–7 min, before any session)

Dynamic only. No static holds before you lift or run.
  1. Cat-cow × 10
  2. World's greatest stretch × 5/side (lunge with rotation)
  3. Hip CARs (controlled articular rotations) × 5/direction/side
  4. Leg swings front-back × 10/leg
  5. Leg swings side-to-side × 10/leg
  6. Bodyweight squats × 10
  7. Inchworm to push-up × 5

Splits routine (20–25 min, post-workout or rest day)

Long holds. Breathe deeply. Achy is OK, sharp is not.
  1. Pigeon pose — 90 sec / side (deep glute + hip)
  2. Couch stretch — 90 sec / side (hip flexor — critical for front splits)
  3. Half split (back knee down, front leg straight) — 90 sec / side
  4. Full front split prep — 60–90 sec / side at your current ROM
  5. Pancake fold (seated wide-leg forward fold) — 90–120 sec
  6. Cossack squat — 8–10 reps / side (loaded mobility — bonus strength)
  7. PNF on hamstrings — 4 rounds: 6s contract → 30s deeper stretch
  8. 90/90 hip stretch — 60 sec / side

Daily success rules

  • Heat first — warm shower or post-workout when muscles are already warm
  • Long holds — 60–120s, no bouncing
  • Daily > intense — better to do 15 min every day than 60 min twice a week
  • Don't push through sharp pain — sharp = back off. Achy = OK.
  • Track ROM weekly — phone video at same angle every Sunday. Visible progress = motivation.
  • Magnesium glycinate at night (already in your stack) — muscle relaxation aids tissue remodeling
  • 7+ hrs sleep — connective tissue remodeling happens during deep sleep

Where these numbers come from (scholar citations)

Zero numbers from USDA dietary guidelines or FDA recommendations. Every target traces to peer-reviewed meta-analyses by independent academic researchers:

  • Distribute 30–40g × 4–5 meals: Schoenfeld & Aragon 2018, J Int Soc Sports Nutr 15(10). The canonical "how much protein per meal" review.
  • Anabolic window is 4–6 hours, not 30 min: Aragon & Schoenfeld 2013, J Int Soc Sports Nutr 10(5). Why pre-/post-workout timing matters less than total daily intake.
  • Carbs 4–7 g/kg: Helms, Aragon, Fitschen 2014, J Int Soc Sports Nutr 11(20). Natural bodybuilding contest prep.

Morning routine (Huberman-aligned)

1. Sunlight 5–10 min within 1 hr of waking — even cloudy days. Regulates cortisol awakening response → better evening sleep. (Huberman / Czeisler)

2. 16–32 oz water + Celtic salt + potassium (or LMNT) — restores fasted-state electrolytes. You're already doing this.

3. Perfect Amino on wake — keeps fasted MPS protected without breaking the fast meaningfully. (Aragon-style "amino spike" tactic)

4. Delay caffeine 90–120 min after wake — lets cortisol peak naturally; reduces afternoon crash. (Huberman)

5. No cold plunge / ice bath within 6 hrs after lifting — attenuates hypertrophy. Save cold for rest days or boxing-only days. (Roberts 2015)

The science (current consensus)

1. Total protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day. Beyond ~1.8 g/kg the muscle-growth curve flattens. We sit at 1.9 g/kg — safe upper end. (Morton 2018 meta · 49 studies)

2. Distribute protein across 4–5 meals at 30–40g each. 24-hr MPS is higher with even spacing than skewed bolus. Each meal needs ≥2.5g leucine (≈30g animal protein). (Schoenfeld 2018 meta · Areta 2013)

3. Pre-sleep slow-release protein (30–40g casein) boosts overnight MPS ~22%. Cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, or casein shake. (Trommelen 2016 · Snijders 2015)

4. Carb floor 4 g/kg/day for hypertrophy + activity. Below 280g you'll feel boxing performance drop within ~5 days. (Helms 2014)

5. Surplus +250–400 kcal, gain 0.25–0.5 lb/week. Faster gain = mostly fat. (Iraki/Helms 2019)

6. Creatine 5 g/day → +2–4 lb LBM in 8 weeks. Most-validated supplement in human nutrition. (Kreider 2017 · ISSN position stand)
